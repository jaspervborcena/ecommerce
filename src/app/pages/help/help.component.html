Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I place an order?

Third-Party Delivery Apps (GrabFood, Foodpanda, DoorDash, Uber Eats): If you ordered through a third-party app, open the app, go to Orders, select your Shao Tang transaction, and click Help or Contact Support. This connects you directly to platform support for missing items, delivery status, or refund requests.

Direct Merchant Website / Online Store: If you ordered directly from a proprietary Shao Tang website, check the website header or footer for a Contact Us, FAQ, or Help Center link, or review your order confirmation email for direct customer service contact details.

How to Watch Out for Promos and Discounts

1. Follow Their Official Social Media Pages
Check Facebook and Instagram for "Shao Tang" (or the specific branch location near you). Local branches post limited-time vouchers, holiday bundle offers, ordering guides, and grand opening discounts directly on their feed and stories.
